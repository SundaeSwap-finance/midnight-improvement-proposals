**dApp name:** SundaeSwap Capacity Exchange — Registry

**Contract repository:** https://github.com/SundaeSwap-finance/capacity-exchange/tree/main/packages/registry/contract

**Brief description:**

An on-chain registry of SundaeSwap Capacity Exchange Service (CES) servers. CES operators publish their public network endpoint (IP address and port) on-chain so clients can find currently active CES servers without needing some off-chain flow.

To register, an operator calls `registerServer` with their endpoint and an expiry timestamp and locks a fixed amount of NIGHT as collateral. Each entry is keyed by the `persistentHash` of an operator-held secret and the secret never goes on-chain. The contract also enforces socket-address uniqueness, so two entries can't claim the same IP+port at once. An entry's expiry can't be set further out than `maximumRegistrationPeriod`, which is fixed at contract deployment.

Operators can extend their registration via `renewRegistration` and reclaim their collateral via `deregisterServer` (which checks that the caller's secret matches the entry's key). Once an entry's expiry has passed, that ownership check is skipped: anyone can call `deregisterServer` to remove the expired entry and send the collateral to a recipient of their choice. Cleanup is permissionless by design so that operators are economically incentivized to renew their registration before expiry and anyone is incentivized to claim collateral from entries that have lapsed.
